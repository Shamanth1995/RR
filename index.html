About us

Welcome to Coorg River Rest, your serene escape into the heart of nature.

Founded by Shive Chokira and Anthra Chokira, our retreat was born out of a passion for hospitality and a deep love for the breathtaking landscapes of Coorg. Nestled amidst lush, sprawling coffee estates, Coorg River Rest offers a perfect blend of tranquility and natural beauty.

Waking up here means opening your doors to magnificent hill views and the soothing sounds of a flowing river that wraps around our property. Whether you are looking to unwind with a walk through the aromatic coffee plantations, listen to the gentle rhythm of the river, or simply soak in the misty mountain vistas, we provide a peaceful sanctuary away from the hustle of everyday life.

Come experience the warmth of Coorgi hospitality, where nature meets comfort, and every moment is a breath of fresh air.
